A chipmunk who serves as protector of the woods near the Great Mushroom in the Feywild. Chippy vibrates with energy too large for his body.

Met at El's fairy ring feast, where he was introduced alongside the other inhabitants. He gifted the party a perfectly preserved fish, wrapped with the care of something precious.
