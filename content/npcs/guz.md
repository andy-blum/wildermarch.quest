name: Guz
disposition: hostile
alive: false
---

A very large orc hired by Ahmed Noke to track down the transmuted Finothere. Guz introduced himself with the authority of someone who has never been told no, claiming the escaped sheep as his own. He fought with an enormous wooden staff topped by a plow-blade and traveled with two transmuted wolves that moved with mechanical precision.

Quick's brambles and Kouzlo's flames disintegrated the wolves. Jorath's serpent magic envenomed Guz, but he fought through it long enough to seize Aldric by the throat and drag him into the river. The transmuted Finothere — still a sheep — saved the paladin with a well-timed headbutt that toppled the orc. The party left him incapacitated in the forest north of the Great Tree.

He was particularly precious about his hairline.
