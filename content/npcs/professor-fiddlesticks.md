An eccentric gnome artificer with wild hair and goggles, constantly working on unstable brews in his small ramshackle hut. P. Fiddlesticks and his assistant Farrim are often on edge, their experiments producing unpredictable results.

When the party mentioned they would venture into the Feywild, he reacted sharply: "Did they give you anything else? Do anything else to you?" His suspicion of the fey runs deep.

He brewed two unstable potions for the Feywild expedition - hot to the touch, fizzing dangerously - that function as Protection from Evil and Good (Fey-specific). Drink at your own risk.

Known for gifting healing potions to newcomers before they set foot beyond camp. He formed a quiet bond with Rowan Swiftleaf.
