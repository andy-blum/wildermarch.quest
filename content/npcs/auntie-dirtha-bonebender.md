A hag who first appeared at Grit's camp to collect her cauldron — seven feet tall, hunched, and carried on swirling dust. She is known to the court records of ancient Ramkheret as *She Who Speaks to Empty Places*, the woman of no noble house who married the Lord of the Eternal Sand three hundred years ago and has been walking the desert ever since.

She rules the western desert from a shack built atop the Sunken Pyramid in a vast cavern beneath the oasis, where she stirs a cauldron of stew that smells like durian, dirty socks, and burning refuse.

**Her Nature:**
Dirtha was mortal once — a merchant's daughter from the eastern reaches who walked into the desert with nothing. She found something there. Something answered when she spoke her name to the deep sand, and she came back with everything. She bound herself to Ramkheret the king and gave him years — three hundred of them — but she kept forever for herself. When he finally tried to break free, she released him rather than keep him bound any longer. "Men. Even dead ones."

**Her Reach:**
- Travels on the wind and in the water; can be at the oasis at any time
- Weaves mirages through dust elementals to lure travelers off course
- Transforms living creatures into other forms — lizards, tortoises, birds — and binds them around her oasis
- Her gaze can be felt across the desert; she "loves the ones who think it was their idea"
- She speaks to empty places and they answer

**The Bargain:**
Grit is bound to her by a deal involving raw magic and potions brewed from cactid remains. His time is running out. Madam Helena has been singled out for special attention since their first encounter at Grit's camp.

**The Game:**
Dirtha trades in deals — *"One truth for one thing freely given. You choose I give, a deal's a deal."* When the party reached her shack she proposed questions traded one for one, then drew bone cards over Helena and Aldric, summoning a giant scorpion and a mummy bound to each. The third card, Judgment, she put away — *"That one's not for today"* — but it carried with it primal terror and the words *"The Empty Throne. I was waiting. I was always waiting. Lucky for you, they sent a herald."*

She dissolved into desert wind, leaving the party on the apex of the Sunken Pyramid with the entrance to the depths below.

**Warning:** Dirtha has now confronted Madam Helena directly, and serves something even older that waits at the Empty Throne.
