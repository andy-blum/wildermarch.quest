A snail who manages travel in the woods near the Great Mushroom in the Feywild. O'Shello carries himself with the dignified pace of someone who refuses to be rushed, raising his eyestalk with modest authority.

Met at El's fairy ring feast. He introduced himself as the one in charge of travel in these woods — said with the quiet confidence of someone whose job matters and who knows it.

He gifted the party a rock. A nice one.
