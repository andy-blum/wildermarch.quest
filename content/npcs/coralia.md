An ocean dryad and guardian of the Coral Coast, translucent as a jellyfish with feet that leave no prints on wet sand. She saved survivors from the shipwreck on the corrupted reef and enlisted the party's help to stop the unnatural poisoning of her domain.

Coralia lost her left forearm in battle with a corrupted creature she called the Leviathan—a former friend turned monstrous. Aldric restored her arm with Tyr's blessing, and in gratitude she named the party "Reef Friends," giving them coral fragments as tokens of recognition among sea guardians.
