A Fey Eladrin who appeared to the party in a temporary pocket of the Feywild within the Great Tree. One of three Eladrin guardians who explained that the Tree exists across three realms—the Material Plane, the Feywild, and the Shadowfell—and that the barrier between planes is failing.
