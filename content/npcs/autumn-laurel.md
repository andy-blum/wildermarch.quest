A Fey Eladrin who appeared to the party in a temporary pocket of the Feywild within the Great Tree. He speaks for the three Eladrin guardians and bowed deeply upon the party's return, calling them "our champions."

Autumn revealed crucial information about the anchor network: the Great Tree is not the only portal site. The Great Pine in the north and the Great Mushroom in the western swamplands are also attunable and connected.

He claimed the Shadowfell incursion at the Great Tree was the first of its kind - though Trogeth's testimony contradicts this. When asked about the cult and psionic creatures on the Material Plane, Autumn faltered, describing them as "mortal threats" he did not recognize.

During negotiations, Madam Helena's Detect Thoughts revealed two names echoing in his mind: Titania and Queen Mab. Summer and Winter. The tension was unmistakable - not deception, but unease.

Thalen claimed a lock of Autumn's golden hair as his promised prize; the Eladrin sliced it free without hesitation. It now threads through Thalen's bowstring.

Autumn promised to consult his brother about the cult pendant the chwingas carved into bark.
