---
name: The Cat
location: sunken-pyramid
disposition: neutral
alive: true
---

A long black cat with amber eyes that rests at the deepest known level of the Sunken Pyramid, in a chamber surrounded by six clay cat statues posed in eternal stillness. The cat speaks in all languages at once, a phenomenon that suggests either ancient magic or a nature that exists partially outside the mortal world.

When the party descended into its chamber, the cat's first word was a simple question: *"Visitors?"*

Its age and origin remain unknown. It may be a guardian of the Empty Throne, or merely a witness to whatever has dwelt in the depths beneath the desert for untold centuries.
