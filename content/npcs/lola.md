A pixie at El's fairy ring feast near the Great Mushroom in the Feywild. Lola handles the menu and food descriptions with obvious pride.

When she spoke out of turn during introductions, El's cane came down lightly on the table with a quiet "Lola. We've talked about this." Her face registered one flash of naked fear before smoothing back to cheerfulness. Quick noticed.

She gifted the party a cookie for dessert, and looked at it regretfully as she handed it over.
