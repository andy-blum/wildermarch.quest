A Shadar-kai spirit bound within the necromancer's journal recovered from beneath the Great Tree. The party parleyed with Trogeth to learn of other failed Shadowfell portals and the Shadar-kai's tragic curse. After surviving a brutal "test of strength" conjured from the book, the party earned knowledge and scrolls of revivify.
