A female lizardfolk and now the leader of what remains of her tribe after cultists from the Cult of the Whispering Stars slaughtered their village in the Pink Swamps and took survivors prisoner.

The party freed Kessara and other lizardfolk from cages in an underground crystal chamber beneath the swamp. She addressed the party with quiet gratitude and asked if they had found her village. Aldric delivered the news as gently as he could.

Kessara explained that the cultists had been extracting "stars from beneath the earth" — unrefined crystals connected to a fallen star from long ago. The star was the source of the swamp's life and abundance, and the pink trees fed off its power.

She and the surviving lizardfolk returned to N.O.D.E. camp with the party.
