A bird living in the Pink Swamps who spoke with Thalen via *Speak with Animals*. Montagne reported that the swamp's inhabitants were small and scaly — lizardfolk — and that they were lying on the ground. He also mentioned that others in dark clothes had recently passed through.

When Thalen gestured to his cult cloak to ask if the dark-clothed visitors wore something similar, Montagne panicked, assumed Thalen was a cultist, and fled.
