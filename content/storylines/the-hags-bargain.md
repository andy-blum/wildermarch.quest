Auntie Dirtha Bonebender, the hag of the western desert, weaves bargains and transformations across the sand. The hermit Grit is bound to her by a deal that has worn his time thin; Madam Helena has been marked by her gaze; and the oasis itself is a doorway into something far older.

**What is known:**
- Dirtha can travel on the wind and in the water, and may be present at the oasis at any time
- She trades in deals of one truth for one thing freely given — *"You choose I give, a deal's a deal"*
- She transforms living creatures into other forms (lizards, tortoises, birds), binding them around her oasis.
- A circle of warding can break her transformations, and destroying her dust elemental noticibly costs her power
- The candied rose stems found in Grit's alchemical stores turn the eater's voice into razors that draw blood from their own throat for the better part of an hour — Dirtha-aligned hag-craft

**Dirtha's True Name:**
Dirtha was known as *She Who Speaks to Empty Places* — a woman born in the merchant quarter of the eastern reaches who walked into the desert with nothing three hundred years ago and came back with everything. She spoke her name to the deep sand, and the deep sand answered. She bound herself to King Ramkheret, giving him years but keeping forever for herself.

**The Reading:**
Dirtha greeted the party at the lake-cavern below the oasis with a deck of bone cards. Each card summoned a creature bound to its target:
- **The Tower, inverted** — *"The locust, ruin that was uninvited"* — a giant scorpion (Kishep the Iron Claw), bound to Helena
- **The Hanged Man** — *"Drowned king. He who would not give, so everything was taken"* — a mummy (Karumon the Bound), bound to Aldric
- **Judgment** — *"The Empty Throne. I was waiting. I was always waiting. Lucky for you, they sent a herald"* — a wave of primal terror, but the card was put away. *"That one's not for today."*

**The Pyramid and Its Secrets:**
Beneath Dirtha's shack lies a vast, ancient pyramid buried under the desert sands. Its existence was hidden until the wind of her departure stripped the cavern floor away. The party defeated the creatures left to defend it and pressed deeper, discovering:

- A marriage contract binding Ramkheret to She Who Speaks to Empty Places, witnessed by the high priests and sealed with promises written in Fey
- The tomb of the king himself, empty save for his journal, inscribed partly in his hand and partly in hers — a record of three centuries' waiting and her final release of him
- The voices of the desert, remembering her as it remembers all that walks upon it
- Twin sphinxes, Sekahnu and Vetherek, guarding the descent further
- A mysterious black cat at the deepest level, speaking in all languages at once
- A bone card appearing in Helena's deck unbidden — the High Priestess, inverted

**The Empty Throne:**
Whatever waits at the Empty Throne is something Dirtha serves. The pyramid itself is a temple to something far older, buried beneath the sands where the desert remembers everything.
