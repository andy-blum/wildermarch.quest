Following the defeat of Ahmed Noke and the restoration of Finothere Shinebright, N.O.D.E. has been offered the wreckage of Shinebright's Treehouse as a forward facility north of the Great Tree. The site could serve as a library, training ground, or full bastion for the growing expedition.

**Open threads:**
- The treehouse is structurally damaged — walls blown out, roof gone, much of the interior scorched. Salvage and rebuild work is required before it can serve as a base.
- Noke claims he can recreate the workshop's teleportation rooms to link the site directly to N.O.D.E. camp. He admits this will be difficult.
- Noke joins the camp on a probationary basis, free to leave at any time. The wizards Jorath and Kouzlo have offered him collaboration in place of the master/apprentice dynamic he resented.
- Finothere has departed to begin again elsewhere, leaving the lineage of his clan and the fate of his work behind.
- Finothere bestowed upon the party the title **B.A.A.** — meaning yet to be revealed.
