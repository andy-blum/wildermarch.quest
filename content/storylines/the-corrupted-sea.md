The coastal waters of the Wildermarch are dying. The reef has turned white and fossilized, sea guardians have been corrupted, and necromantic crystals transform the dead into sea spawn.

**Key Figures:**
- **Coralia** - Ocean dryad and guardian of the Coral Coast. She was dragged beneath the waves by a massive octopus during the reef rescue. Her fate is unknown.
- **The Leviathan** - Once a guardian and friend to Coralia, now corrupted and hostile. Coralia lost her arm fighting it (later restored by Aldric).
- **The Psionic Abomination** - A vast creature with four glowing red eyes and innumerable tentacles that surfaced after the Coral Forest battle, watching with fury before fleeing into the deep.

**What is Known:**
- At least two leviathan-class entities exist beneath the coast
- The corruption coincides with N.O.D.E.'s arrival, though its origins predate them
- Necromantic crystals grow stronger as creatures die nearby, transforming the dead into sea spawn
- Cultist bodies near the crystals retained some abilities after transformation
- The mind control effect from the psionic creature matches the obelisk found on the bluff

**Progress:**
- The Coral Forest crystal was destroyed, partially cleansing that section of coast
- The psionic abomination fled into the deep, radiating fury
- Coralia gave her trident to Ozemon and her cloak to Jorath before being taken
- The party earned the title "Reef Friend" and carries coral fragments as tokens

**The Aboleth Connection:**
The interrogation of Silas confirmed that the psionic abomination in the Coral Forest was one of multiple Aboleths — "one of my brothers." The party has also slain at least one Aboleth, described as "an unforgivable act." Lizardfolk sailors aboard the Tidefang reported deep-beasts that crawl inside minds and whisper without words, causing crew to hurl themselves overboard and return hollowed out — classic Aboleth behavior.

**Unresolved:**
- Coralia's fate remains unknown
- The underwater tower depicted in cult drawings has not been explored
- S'shar and the Tidefang are missing at sea, likely encountering Aboleths
