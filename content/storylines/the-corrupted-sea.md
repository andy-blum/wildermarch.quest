The coastal waters of the Wildermarch are dying. The reef has turned white and fossilized, sea guardians have been corrupted, and necromantic crystals transform the dead into sea spawn.

**Key figures:**
- Coralia, ocean dryad and guardian of the Coral Coast, lost her arm fighting the Leviathan
- The Leviathan, once a guardian and friend to Coralia, now corrupted and hostile
- A four-eyed psionic abomination connected to the cult

**What is known:**
- At least two leviathan-class entities exist beneath the coast
- The corruption coincides with N.O.D.E.'s arrival, though its origins predate them
- Ships continue to wreck on the dying reef
- Coralia has named the party "Reef Friends" and will visit camp to discuss further

**Progress:**
- The Coral Forest crystal was destroyed, cleansing that section of coast
- The psionic abomination fled into the deep after watching the battle
- Coralia's arm was restored by Aldric's divine magic
