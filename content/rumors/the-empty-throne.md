When Dirtha drew the Judgment card during her bone-card reading, her voice shifted — lower, older — and she spoke of something she had been waiting for:

> *"The Empty Throne. I was waiting. I was always waiting. Lucky for you, they sent a herald."*

A wave of primal terror crashed over Helena and Aldric — a dread beyond reason, beyond courage, the certainty that they stood before something ancient and patient and utterly without mercy. Dirtha tucked the card away. *"That one's not for today."*

The hag is not the apex of this thread. She serves something deeper, and the party has been named its herald — whether they wanted to be or not. Whatever sits on the Empty Throne, Dirtha has been waiting for it for a very long time.

Whether the Empty Throne is connected to the cult's central eye — the elder Aboleth that "saw everything be made" — or is something else entirely is not yet known.
