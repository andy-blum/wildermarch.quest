Coralia visited the N.O.D.E. camp as promised, arriving from the shoreline with salt and coral clinging faintly to her form. She arrived during the unlocking of Trogeth's journal and immediately recognized the Shadowfell danger, confirming the necromancers were different from the cultists - different magic, different intent.

She stayed to witness the journal's test and helped shake the party back to awareness after the trial of combat.

**Note:** Coralia's current fate is unknown. She was dragged beneath the waves by a massive octopus during a subsequent reef rescue mission.
