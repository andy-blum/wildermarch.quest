When the party climbed to Grit's mountain camp before crossing the desert, the camp was empty — the ornate tent undisturbed, the great cauldron dry and cold. Grit himself was nowhere to be found.

Among his alchemical stores, Kouzlo identified candied rose stems treated with arcane powder; anyone who eats one finds their voice turned to razors that draw blood from their own throat for nearly an hour. Hag-craft, kept among Grit's things.

Given the bargain that binds him and Dirtha's reminder of *"tick tock,"* his absence is alarming. Whether he is hiding, taken, or dispatched on the hag's errands is unknown.
