When the party destroyed the lava-fed red crystal in the mountain cavern, they received a chilling shared vision:

A vast psionic empire beneath the waves acknowledged them—and promised it was coming.

This was not a threat from a single creature or cult cell. Something immense and organized lurks in the deep, and it now knows the party exists.
