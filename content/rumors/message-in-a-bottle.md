Inside the washed-ashore sea serpent, Lorvial found a sealed bottle containing a note written in a Draconic script. The party was unable to translate the message immediately.

Mel translated the Draconic letter in Session 20. It was a love note from S'shar of the Tidefang — captain of the lizardfolk's largest vessel — to Kessara. S'shar wrote of the sea's cruelty, of deep-beasts that crawl inside the mind and whisper without words, and of his hope of returning home to hatch a clutch together. The "deep-beasts" almost certainly describe the Aboleths.

In Session 27, N.O.D.E. found the Tidefang in a tidal sea cave south of camp. S'shar and his surviving crew had retreated to the cave's deepest chamber, where lead-rich galena in the stone dulled the aboleth's whispers, and were killed by a chuul thrall before they could sail out. The party carried the bodies home to Kessara.
