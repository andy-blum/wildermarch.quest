During the exploration south of camp, the party noted a series of sea caves near the beach where they fought giant crabs. The caves were made inaccessible by the tides at the time.

**Resolved (Session 27):** The tidal cave beside the sea-beast's bones was entered at low tide on N.O.D.E.'s expedition to find the Tidefang. The ship was inside, half-afloat in the shallows, alongside the camp and cairns of its lost crew. The party fought through giant crabs and a giant spider in the entrance pool, then a chuul thrall guarding the deep chamber, and recovered the bodies of S'shar and his lizardfolk crew. The Tidefang was sailed back along the coast to camp.
