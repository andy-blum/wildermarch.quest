During the exploration south of camp, the party noted a series of sea caves near the beach where they fought giant crabs. The caves were made inaccessible by the tides at the time.

These caves warrant further investigation when conditions allow. Given the cult's presence throughout coastal areas, they may contain threats—or treasures.
