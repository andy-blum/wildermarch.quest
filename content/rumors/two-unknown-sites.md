The two sites referenced in the necromancer's journal have been identified through communion with the Shadar-kai spirit Trogeth and confirmation from the Fey Eladrin:

- **The Great Pine** - Northern mountains, dense forest, snow-covered ground, cliffs, dim overcast skies
- **The Great Mushroom** - Western swamplands, towering fungal giants, vibrant wetlands

Both were previous Shadowfell portal attempts, abandoned due to their dangers. They remain unexplored by the party, though the Fey Eladrin have shown them through scrying.
