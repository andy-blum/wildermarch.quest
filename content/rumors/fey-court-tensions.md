During negotiations with the Fey Eladrin, Madam Helena's Detect Thoughts skimmed the surface of Autumn Laurel's mind. Two names surfaced repeatedly:

- **Titania** - Queen of the Summer Court
- **Queen Mab** - Queen of the Winter Court

The tension between these powers was unmistakable - not deception from the Eladrin, but unease. The guardians of the Great Tree may be caught between larger political forces in the Feywild.

When asked about the cult and psionic creatures on the Material Plane, Autumn faltered and described them as "mortal threats" he did not recognize - or could not admit to recognizing.
