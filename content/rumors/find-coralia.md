Coralia of the Coral Coast was dragged beneath the waves by a massive octopus during the reef rescue mission. Her final words: "Go. You always have a friend on the reef."

She gave her trident to Ozemon and her cloak to Jorath before being taken. Her fate remains unknown.

The party owes her a debt - she healed their wounds, saved shipwrecked sailors, and named them Reef Friends. If she lives, she needs rescue. If she does not, the sea guardians deserve to know what happened to their sister.
