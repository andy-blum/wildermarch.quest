---
name: Chwinga
type: fey
threat: low
faction: guardians-of-the-great-tree
---

Tiny fey forest spirits that inhabit the area around the Great Tree. Chwingas are shy but curious, communicating through gestures rather than speech. They guided the expedition to the Great Tree and have shown gratitude through gifts of runed stones imbued with heroism.

Corrupted or tainted versions of chwingas have been encountered near sources of magical pollution, capable of summoning elementals.
