Insectoid creatures dwelling in a tunnel network beneath the Moon Corn fields. They produce corrosive acidic goo that dissolves shoes and equipment, and have been responsible for the disappearance of cattle - including Betty and Bernice.

The party discovered their nest after Elarie fell into a mound of dirt concealing a tunnel entrance. Multiple insects attacked, along with a larger "mama" variant. The nest was destroyed and one cow was recovered, but the bugkin Roland Poland warned of more creatures deeper below.

Jorath brought an egg back to camp for study.
