---
name: Dirtha's Eels
type: monstrosity
threat: high
---

Two of Auntie Dirtha Bonebender's favorite creatures — enormous eels, each a hundred feet of sinuous muscle and teeth, patrolling the vertical shaft beneath the oasis. They guard the descent into the cavern below the desert.

The two are named **Snitches** and **Stitches**, and they bicker constantly:

> *"Stitches — I think we have intruders."*
> *"Snitches, you're always telling fibs."*

Their squabbling allowed the party to slip past on the way down. When the eels finally noticed, Dirtha herself sent a bolt of light arcing through the water — but it arrived too late.
