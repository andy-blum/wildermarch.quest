---
name: The Leviathan
type: monstrosity
threat: deadly
faction: sea-guardians
---

A corrupted former guardian of the sea, once a friend to Coralia the ocean dryad. Now twisted into a tentacled sea serpent of immense size and malevolence. The Leviathan tore away Coralia's arm in battle and represents one of at least two leviathan-class entities lurking beneath the coast.

The creature's corruption coincides with the cult's activities in the region.
