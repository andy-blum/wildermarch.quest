---
name: Assassin Bug
type: monstrosity
threat: medium
---

Smaller flight-capable insects that hunt the bugkin colonists with precision and stealth. They produce acidic secretions and can attempt to inject something into their victims - possibly venom or paralytic agents.

These creatures attack silently, dropping from ceilings or emerging from darkness. Like the Ankegs, they show no signs of intelligence or capacity for communication. Mahnrag tore one's arm off and used it to skewer the creature.
