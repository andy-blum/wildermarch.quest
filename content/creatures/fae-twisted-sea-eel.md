---
name: Fae-Twisted Sea Eel
type: fey
threat: medium
faction: cult-of-the-whispering-stars
---

Wolf-sized eels corrupted by fae magic, encountered in the bleached Coral Forest. They burst from fog whispering in Sylvan: "Feed the crystal" and "Blood for the heartbeat." These creatures served the necromantic crystal that was transforming the dead into sea spawn.
