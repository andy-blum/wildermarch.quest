---
name: Dust Elemental
type: elemental
threat: medium
---

A towering creature of spinning grit and fury, summoned by Auntie Dirtha Bonebender to weave illusions across the open desert. The party encountered one luring them toward a mirage of the oasis — a shimmering false target that remained fixed on the horizon while real landmarks drifted past.

The illusion peeled away when the elemental was struck, revealing the spinning column of sand beneath. Its destruction shattered the mirage entirely and visibly cost Dirtha some small measure of her power.
