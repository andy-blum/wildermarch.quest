---
name: Sea Spawn
type: monstrosity
threat: medium
faction: cult-of-the-whispering-stars
---

Twisted humanoid creatures that serve the underwater cult. Sea spawn are created when bodies are transformed by necromantic crystals—the dead liquify and are reborn as warped reflections of what they once were.

Stronger variants retain magical abilities from their previous lives, such as frost magic and teleportation. They have been encountered in the northern sea cave, the corrupted cave, and the Coral Forest.
