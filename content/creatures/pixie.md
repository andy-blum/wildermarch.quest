---
name: Pixie
type: fey
threat: low
---

Tiny fey creatures with wings, tiny bows, and blow darts. They giggle invisibly and attack with spores and enchantments, treating combat as sport rather than survival.

Encountered in the Feywild near the Great Mushroom, pixies descended on the party with darts and sparkling dust that caused Madam Helena and Kouzlo to collapse. They revealed themselves only to mock their prey - until something pounced from the grass and devoured one of their number.

The pixies vanished when Displacer Beasts emerged, but returned to celebrate the party's victory as if the whole affair had been entertainment.
