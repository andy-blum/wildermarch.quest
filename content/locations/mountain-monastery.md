name: Mountain Monastery
type: dungeon

A small stone chapel and warren of corridors built into the flank of a mountain north of the Great Tree, well past the wizards' tower. The facade is carved with the symbols of **the Order of the Long Death** — a defunct death-cult monastic line that served Jergul and his kind, and is said in dwarven oral history to have died out after one of its houses made contact with something from the Underdark. The construction itself matches what was once the work of the *Monks of the Vale*.

Ahmed Noke discovered the building during the months he spent in the Wildermarch before N.O.D.E. arrived. He never went inside. He reported a wailing sound near the structure that seemed to come from inside his own head, and the presence of animals he was certain had been dead.

**Approach:**
The forest around the monastery has been infested. Creatures that should be dead walk in jerky, aimless patterns — eye sockets hollow, with fine pale fibers feathering out around the orbits and threading through any old wounds. The animals are puppeted by a mycelium that re-knits their hides and resists fire only weakly. At range, with torches held to the ground, the growth is endurable.

**The Sanctuary:**
A single chamber inside the doorway, glossy with mycelium that lies across stone in a thin oil-slick film. A stag the party tracked here sat in the center of the room, watching a stone door at the far end of a hallway and waiting.

**The Star Door:**
A carved stone door at the end of the sanctuary's hallway. Twenty-three stars are cut into its face, eight drilled all the way through. The inscription reads: *"We who kept the darkness below looked always to the light above. What cannot be held in the hand, only in the eye."* The door opens when the three star-holes through which light passes — a hidden triangle — are simultaneously covered. The party solved this.

**The Iron Corridor:**
Beyond the star door, the hallway narrows to six feet and the mycelium thickens from a sheen to a fibrous gray mat. The air grows heavy. Whispers begin to take from those who linger — a familiar voice screaming in the mind, a name forgotten, a song's final bar lost, a slowly rising white-noise static. The corridor ends at an unlocked iron door.

**The Inner Chamber (not yet entered):**
Glimpsed through the keyhole. A great pale biomass the size of a horse, white shot with small purple dots, stands at the room's center. Two figures stand before it, very close to the door — a monk in robes with his mouth held open by a plume of mycelium, and a woman in leather armor with a sword at her hip. She is the nearer. She is not breathing. When Gryph dropped his tipper in the corridor, she turned her head toward the sound.

The same voice that called itself ***MY COLLECTION GROWS*** through the static of the stag's mind appears to operate from somewhere inside this place.
