A towering, ancient tree deep in the eastern forest that exists simultaneously across three realms: the Material Plane, the Feywild, and the Shadowfell. Home to the chwingas and protected by Fey Eladrin guardians.

The Tree suffered corruption from necromantic energy seeping through from the Shadowfell, channeled by crystals operated by necromancers. The party has partially healed the damage, but the transport between realms remains unstable. An undead ice dragon lurks in the Shadowfell depths beneath the Tree, and something ancient has noticed the resistance to its influence.

Golden light now spreads through the bark and roots where healing has taken hold.
