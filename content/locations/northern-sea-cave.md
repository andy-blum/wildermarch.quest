A mist-shrouded cliffside cave north of camp, visible once fog cleared near the cliffs. The cave features a treacherous 200-foot cliff climb inhabited by Piercers and a deadly Roper.

At the summit, the party discovered a powerful psychic obelisk and glimpsed the underwater red-stone tower—evidence that the cult's influence extends far beyond the caves below. Mist stones with teleportation properties and fungi samples were recovered.
