name: The Pink Swamps
type: wilderness
status: rumored

---

Vibrant pink-hued swamplands to the south, visible from the mountains. Home to lizardfolk who the hermit Grit described as "good people."

A letter recovered from cultists in the corrupted cave mentioned reinforcements delayed by lizardfolk attacks, suggesting the lizardfolk actively oppose cult activity in their territory.
