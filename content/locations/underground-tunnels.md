A network of tunnels beneath the Moon Corn fields, discovered while investigating missing livestock and corrosive goo damaging the crops. Strange moving shapes and dirt mounds on the surface led to the entrance.

The party battled insectoid creatures including a larger "mama" monster, destroyed their nest, and recovered one of the missing cows. A bugkin named Roland "Rolly" Poland warned of more creatures deeper below. Jorath recovered a mysterious egg for study.
