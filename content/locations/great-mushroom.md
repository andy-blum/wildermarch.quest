One of three anchor points connecting the Material Plane to the Feywild and Shadowfell, located in the western swamplands.

The Fey Eladrin revealed this location when showing the party the portal network beneath the Great Tree. The scrying pool displayed towering fungal giants, vibrant and enormous, with wetlands dotted with pools.

The Shadar-kai spirit Trogeth mentioned the Mushroom as one of two other portal sites attempted before the Great Tree - both abandoned due to their dangers.

The party was violently expelled into a vibrant field of flowers near the Mushroom after Trogeth's journal interfered with their transit through the Feywild. Songbirds sang and warm radiance surrounded them, but pixies immediately attacked with darts and spores, followed by Displacer Beasts emerging from the grass.

The rounded shape of the Great Mushroom was visible cresting a distant ridge.
