---
name: The Wizard's Tower
type: landmark
status: rumored
---

A leaning wizard's tower crowned over a great lake, spotted from the southern mountains by the hermit Grit.

Grit pointed out this landmark when describing the visible terrain from his desert camp, along with an oasis to the west and the pink swamps where friendly lizardfolk dwell to the south.

The tower's origin and current occupant, if any, remain unknown.
