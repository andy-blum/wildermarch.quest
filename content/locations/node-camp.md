The expedition's beachhead settlement in the Wildermarch. What began as ramshackle tents and desperation has grown into a proper outpost with fields of moon corn, a converted ship serving as a permanent kitchen, an aqueduct system bringing fresh water from the river, and a flag bearing the sigil of N.O.D.E.

Key figures include Captain Marinn who coordinates exploration, Chef Simon who runs the kitchen, and various sailors rescued from the corrupted reef who now tend the fields.
