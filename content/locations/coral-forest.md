A graveyard of dead coral rising like weaponized bone along the coast, with shipwrecks from forgotten eras embedded among its growth. Coralia the ocean dryad guided the party through by holding back the sea itself, allowing them to walk along the seafloor.

At the forest's heart, a necromantic crystal transformed the dead into sea spawn. The party destroyed it, but not before a massive psionic abomination with four red eyes surfaced to watch—radiating fury before fleeing into the deep.

The coral is razor-sharp and partially bleached white from corruption. Though cleansed, the forest remains deeply scarred.
