A coastal area south of camp where the river empties into the ocean. The party encountered giant crabs here, including a massive variant, while they feasted on the remains of a washed-ashore sea serpent.

Notable discoveries include sea caves made inaccessible by tides and a clean river with unnaturally invigorating water and small glowing creatures at the bottom.
