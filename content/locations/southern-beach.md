A coastal area south of camp where the river empties into the ocean. The party encountered giant crabs here, including a massive variant, while they feasted on the remains of a washed-ashore sea serpent.

Notable discoveries include sea caves made inaccessible by tides and a clean river with unnaturally invigorating water and small glowing creatures at the bottom. The sea-beast's bones still lie where they fell, picked clean by gulls and tides and ringed with the hollow shells of scavenging crabs. The tidal sea cave just beside them — the one that swallowed the Tidefang — was finally entered at low tide and explored to its deepest chamber.
