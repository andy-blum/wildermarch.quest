A castle discovered deep in the tunnels beyond the bugkin colony, visible through a rear entrance of an Ankeg egg chamber. Activity moves around the distant structure — unmistakably cult business.

The party recognized this fortress as part of the slow-dawning geometry of cult activity scattered across the Wildermarch — a constellation of sites they've been stumbling through for weeks. They sealed the tunnel leading to it for now, understanding they were not equipped for that confrontation.

**Confirmed by the Aboleth:**
The subterranean fortress houses the bulk of the Cult of the Whispering Stars' forces, making final preparations for *the unification*. The Aboleth does not normally dwell there but can reach it via the large body of water adjacent to the structure — an ongoing waterway connection to the deep. The time, Silas warned, is nigh.
