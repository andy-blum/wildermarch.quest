An unremarkable empty field beyond the N.O.D.E. outpost that corresponds to the center point of the cult's activity pattern.

When Thalen aligned the Amulet of Whispering Stars with his starry cloak and mapped it against the Wildermarch, a pattern emerged: every major cult encounter, every crystal, every eye - they form a deliberate shape. And at the center of that shape lies this seemingly ordinary field.

The largest eye on the amulet corresponds to this location.

Power does not scatter itself so neatly without purpose.
