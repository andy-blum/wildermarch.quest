A high point north of camp reached after climbing the northern sea cave. At the summit stands a powerful psychic obelisk that projects mind-affecting influence similar to that of the psionic abomination encountered in the Coral Forest.

From here, the party glimpsed the underwater red-stone tower in the distance—confirming the cult's presence extends far beneath the waves.
