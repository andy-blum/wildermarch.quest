A tower beneath the waves with a red crystal roof, seen in cult drawings and glimpsed from the bluff north of camp. The drawings depict larger versions of the psionic aberrations surrounding it.

The tower appears to be central to the cult's operations. Its true nature and the horrors within remain unknown.
