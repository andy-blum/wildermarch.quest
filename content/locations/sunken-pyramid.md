A vast, ancient pyramid buried beneath the desert, descending into the earth from beneath Auntie Dirtha Bonebender's shack. Its apex sits in the sand of an enormous underground cavern; the structure itself plunges far deeper.

**The Cavern:**
The oasis pool is no pool at all — it drops away into a deep vertical shaft, a well plunging into blackness beneath the desert. The shaft is patrolled by Dirtha's eels and emerges into open air through a thundering waterfall that feeds a vast underground lake. Stalagmites and stalactites of brown desert stone frame the chamber, and an ambient glow softens the darkness enough to see without other light.

**Dirtha's Shack:**
On the far bank past a dune of sand sits a small shack adorned with animal bones, with a bubbling cauldron out front producing a smell like durian, dirty socks, and burning refuse. Dirtha can stop the waterfall with a snap of her fingers. The shack does not sit on stone — it sits on the apex of the pyramid, with the entrance leading down into the dark below.

**The Empty Throne:**
When Dirtha drew the Judgment card during her bone-card reading, her voice shifted — lower, older — and she spoke of "the Empty Throne. I was waiting. I was always waiting. Lucky for you, they sent a herald." Whatever waits in the depths of the pyramid is something she serves.

When Dirtha vanished into the wind, the sand began to strip away from the cavern floor grain by grain, revealing the pyramid that had been buried beneath it.
