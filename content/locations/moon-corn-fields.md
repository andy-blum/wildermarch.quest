Fertile fields south of camp where Jorath planted moon corn—a purple grain from the Nocturne Conservatory that thrives in moonlight. The pale stalks have begun to bud under unfamiliar stars, tended by sailors rescued from the reef.

Recently threatened by patches of bubbling green acid that appeared shortly after aqueduct construction. Oxen have gone missing, and investigation revealed underground tunnels beneath the fields inhabited by insectoid creatures.
