A corrupted reef northwest of camp where multiple ships have wrecked, including Captain Embercrest's companion vessel. Sections have turned stark white and fossilized—the vibrant, living reef is dying.

Coralia saved survivors from the wreckage and enlisted the party's help. The corruption here matches signs seen elsewhere: necromantic influence spreading through the coastal waters.
