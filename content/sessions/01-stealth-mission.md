While investigating strange noises near the river north of camp, the party discovered the water was unnaturally oily and viscous, soon encountering eerie green creatures that seemed to flicker in and out of existence and could summon elemental forces from earth and water. After defeating them and watching the corruption lift, the now-peaceful spirits pointed toward a great tree with a cave nearby — the likely source of the river's magical taint.
