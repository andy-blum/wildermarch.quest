## Introduction

An errant knight, exiled from his kingdom after his disgraceful bewithcment by a fairy temptress.

## What We've Learned

A self-styled knight of unmistakably Arthurian bearing who cuts a strange figure even by Wildermarch standards - ivy-woven hair, autumn-tinted skin, and one eye a solid, pupil-less green. Gentle of voice and careful in word, he spent weeks avoiding the camp proper, sleeping in the wilds and trading only when necessary.

During the reef rescue, Sir Ancel fought like a storybook knight dragged into a nightmare, surviving on sheer will after being harpooned and dragged toward the surf. He spoke for the party when negotiating with Coralia, offering aid and recompense with the courtesy of a knight who remembers what such titles once meant.
