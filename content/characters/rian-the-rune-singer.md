Warrior poet raised by Orcs. Rediscovers his love for the elven language through poetry. Seeking to redeem his sordid past with the Ironfang tribe through authoring a heroic final chapter in the Wildermarch.

> "Orcs kill. Humans tell tall tales of their exploits. Elves sing. I do all three, sometimes all at once."
