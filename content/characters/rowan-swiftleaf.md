## Introduction

Always looking for the next... something. Adventure? Sometimes. Challenge? If needed. Snack? Always.

## What We've Learned

A wanderer by nature and by memory - though not all of his life seems accounted for. Rowan came overland through the mountain pass, the Wildermarch the only place left on the far side. He formed a quiet bond with Professor Fiddlesticks, who gifted him a healing potion before he ever set foot beyond camp.

During the Coral Forest expedition, Rowan fell under the grip of a powerful psionic charm. His vision blurred with trees, earth, and the wooded homeland of his youth as a vast presence reached out from beyond the reef. Under its influence, he loosed an arrow at his companions - only Aldric's intervention prevented tragedy. The shock broke the charm's hold, and horror crossed Rowan's face as reality snapped back.

He carries coral-tipped adamantine arrows, a gift from Coralia, and the weight of guilt from that moment.
