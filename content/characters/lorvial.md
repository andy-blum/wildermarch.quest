## Introduction

Haunted by his failings during the riots that broke out during the "Celebration of Luminescence" holiday, he has left the guard and sees this as a chance to self-exile.

> "Don't worry! Calm down, you'll only die if your meant to."

## What We've Learned

A natural leader and organizer, Lorvial insisted on the expedition's core principle after nearly losing party members: "All of us go, and all of us return." He administers demerits for unauthorized magic use in camp - a system that requires further codification.

His mastery of Shape Water has proven invaluable - creating ice canoes to cross deep water, parting mist, and freezing barriers mid-combat. During the northern sea cave expedition, his spell carved pockets of clarity through living fog and saved his companions from being overwhelmed.
