## Introduction

Rory Ashbloom has spent the majority of his life serving as the fire marshall for a large wooded camping community. His days were spent educating youth and adult campers alike about the dangers of poor campfire protocol. Due to some changes at the behest of the marketing team, his position was filled by another druid who always assumes the shape of a grizzly bear in a hat. Undeterred from his life's purpose, he recently has joined up with N.O.D.E. in their conservation initiative to preserve, conserve, and protect the peninsula's lands. Excited for his new role and eager to make coworkers into friends, Rory and his trusty Flamespawn sidekick Bernard have sailed to the Wildermarch.

Just as fire can destroy, it can also be the catalyst for growth and Rory aims to bring the dichotomy of flame to his adventures!
