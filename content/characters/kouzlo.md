## Introduction

Optimistically re-inventing himself as an adventurer.

## What We've Learned

Kouzlo dreams of founding "Kouzlo's Coffee Cartel" - a brewing venture that Aldric has been advocating for with alarming enthusiasm. His morning ritual of brewing arcane coffee has become a cornerstone of camp morale.

Beyond the coffee enterprise, Kouzlo harbors dreams of building his own wizard's tower somewhere in the Wildermarch.

He carries a Stone of Good Luck (a pearl recovered from the northern sea cave) and has become the keeper of Trogeth's journal - a necromancer's tome containing a bound Shadar-kai spirit. He successfully suppressed Trogeth's interference during transit to the Feywild through sheer arcane will.

He identified the glowing fungus Noctiluceum and collected samples for potential underwater breathing potions. His cold logic and remarkable magical output have proven indispensable to the expedition.

From Grit's collection, he received an Oil of Slipperiness.
