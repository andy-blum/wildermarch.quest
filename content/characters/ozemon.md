## What We've Learned

A tiefling sorcerer with shaved horns and a sharp chip on his shoulder. Ozemon arrived at the N.O.D.E. outpost only days before the reef rescue expedition, keeping mostly to himself and burying his nose in books. His fire-leaning magic and quick tongue soon made an impression.

When Coralia was dragged beneath the waves by the massive octopus, she hurled her coral-forged trident onto the deck of the party's ship, calling Ozemon her friend. He now carries her weapon and her charge - a physical representation of the oath to help her.

His lightning tore through clustered enemies during the reef battle with controlled fury. He watches the world as one long accused, measuring whether it will condemn him again.
