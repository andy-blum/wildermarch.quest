## Introduction

Frontier drifter wandering from town to town. Seeking adventure and inspiration for his tall tales and historic ballads.

> "Easy there friend. Let me play you a little song I know and, by the end, you might change your mind."

## What We've Learned

Saim joined the southern expedition with Jorath, Arkose, and Lorvial. During the giant crab battle, he played a magical lullaby to put half the crabs to sleep, demonstrating the practical power of his bardic talents.
