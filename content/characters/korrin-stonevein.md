He served proudly as keeper of the Ember Hall, the heart of his clan's craft. But one fateful night, the forge erupted with unnatural fire, consuming everything. When the smoke cleared, only Korrin stood amid the ashes. His clan saw guilt where he saw only confusion, and he was cast out, marked as Ashwalker.

Now he wanders the frontier - a land whispered to hold ruins touched by the same strange fire. His only guide is a dwarven compass meant to point toward home, though its needle spins madly in his hands. Each ruin, each flicker of that otherworldly flame, feels like a clue - or a warning.

He's no longer sure whether he's chasing redemption... or the same curse that burned his life away.
