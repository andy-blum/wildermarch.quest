Aldric Vaun, once a respected officer in the Royal Guard, uncovered a scandal that threatened the court's stability. In response, he was exiled to the Wildermarch under the guise of a promotion. Now serving with the Noble Order of Discovery and Exploration (N.O.D.E.), Vaun bides his time, holding onto secrets that give him both power and leverage. His ultimate goal is unclear, but he quietly gathers strength and waits for the right moment to return on his own terms.

> "Justice does not hurry. Nor do I. I'll wait as long as I must - then I will not hesitate."
