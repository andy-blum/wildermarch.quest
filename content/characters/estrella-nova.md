## Introduction

Estrella Nova hails from the North where the aurora borealis shines bright. Her people's culture centers around the Northern Lights, which inspires both mystical tradition and scholarly activity. Estrella has followed in the footsteps of her parents as an astronomer. As much as she loves the village of Lunarcta, she has always dreamed of mapping the stars across Wildermarch.
