## Introduction

A weathered forest gnome with a tired, kind smile. "Here to plumb the leylines.", she'll respond if asked why she's along with the expedition. Always has a possum on her shoulder, but you swear there are many beady eyes staring at you when you aren't quite looking.

> "You never know what you might find in the woods. Deep in the oldgrowth they say you might find the seams between the planes, flickering bright like an oil fire. Oh, nevermind me, would you like to play some Three Dragon Ante? The possum doesn't bite."
