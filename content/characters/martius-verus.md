Frontier half-orc turned loyal soldier to the king. Sees the mountains of the Wildermarch as a birthright denied. The Crown allowed him to return for the promise of a title and a mercenary band of his own.

> "I have known a life at the edge of the Law's light. I will bring the Eternal Sun to the Wildermarch."
