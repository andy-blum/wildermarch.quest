## Introduction

On a journey to become an epic hero so that the future of gnomes is not destined to be cheap lawn decoration for humans.

## What We've Learned

A fellow diviner who Madam Helena recognized immediately upon his arrival at camp. Mulchbane arrived bright-eyed and eager to prove himself worthy of N.O.D.E., asking after rumors of gnomes in the region.

He joined the mountain expedition south of camp, where his keen eye for hidden hazards proved invaluable. During the brutal cactid ambush in the canyon, Mulchbane vanished into the dust, striking from shadows. The hermit Grit clapped him on the back after the battle and restored what the cactus creatures had stolen.

He received Gloves of Swimming and Climbing from Grit's collection.
