---
name: Necromancer's Journal
type: wondrous
rarity: rare
---

A mysterious book recovered from the necromancers beneath the Great Tree, written in an unknown script. The journal contains shifting wards and houses the bound spirit of Trogeth, a Shadar-kai.

The party parleyed with Trogeth to learn of other failed Shadowfell portals and the Shadar-kai's tragic curse. After surviving a brutal, reality-warping "test of strength" conjured from the book, they earned knowledge and two scrolls of revivify.

The journal references two additional points of interest whose locations remain undetermined.
