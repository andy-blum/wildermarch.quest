---
name: Wand of Fireballs (Depleting)
type: wand
owner: grit
rarity: rare
origin: mountain-cavern
---

A wand recovered from the shattered red crystal in the mountain cavern. Unlike standard wands of fireballs, this one will not recharge - it had only 7 total uses before becoming an ordinary stick.

The wand was found among the cult's supplies after the party destroyed their ritual site. Jorath Nightbloom carried it for a time, but gifted the wand with its remaining charges to the desert hermit Grit as a sign of friendship during the mountain expedition.
