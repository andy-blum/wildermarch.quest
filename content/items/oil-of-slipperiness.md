---
name: Oil of Slipperiness
type: potion
rarity: uncommon
---

A thick, viscous oil that grants the effects of a Freedom of Movement spell for 8 hours when applied. Alternatively, it can be poured on the ground to create a 10-foot square slick area that functions as difficult terrain.

**Known supplies:**
- One extracted from the saliva of the Roper defeated during the sea cavern climb (Session 7)
- One from the desert hermit Grit's collection, given to Kouzlo (Session 12) — surrendered to the Coffer of Equivalent Exchange in the Sunken Pyramid (Session 25)
