---
name: Immovable Rod
type: wondrous
rarity: uncommon
origin: northern-sea-cave
---

A flat iron rod with a button on one end, recovered from the stomach of the Roper during the sea cavern climb. When activated, the rod becomes magically fixed in place, holding up to 8,000 pounds of weight regardless of gravity.

Shadow has made effective use of the rod during subsequent expeditions, deploying it as an anchor point for descent into the portal pool beneath the Great Tree.
