A small pewter band set with a tiny brain in relief.

Drawn from the Coffer of Equivalent Exchange in the Sunken Pyramid alongside the Bracelet of the Cobra and the Bow of the Horizon. Jorath turned it over once in his hand and passed it to Kouzlo. *"I think you will need this."*

**Properties (requires attunement):**
- The wearer is immune to magic that allows other creatures to read their thoughts, determine whether they are lying, know their alignment, or know their creature type
- The wearer can permit or deny telepathic communication from others
- As an action, the wearer can cause the ring to become invisible until they use another action to make it visible, remove it, or die
- If the wearer dies while wearing the ring, their soul enters it (unless another soul already occupies it). The trapped soul may choose to remain in the ring or depart to the afterlife, and can telepathically contact any creature wearing the ring.
