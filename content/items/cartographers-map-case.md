A magical map case that replaces a standard cartographer's kit. It grants a +2 bonus to relevant checks when mapping terrain or navigating by chart.

A gift from the desert hermit Grit's collection, perfectly suited for the wilderness scout Quick.

In the Feywild, the case produced a living map — not static ink but breathing parchment where rivers thread through painted valleys, miniature trees sway in no wind, and a glowing mark drifts where the party stands. It tracked their progress in real time, serving as a living compass that pulled them true toward the Great Mushroom. The pixies were fascinated by it and used it to guide the party along the correct path.
