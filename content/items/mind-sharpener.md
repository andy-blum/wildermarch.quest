type: wondrous

An attunement item that helps maintain concentration on spells. When the wearer fails a Constitution saving throw to maintain concentration, they can use their reaction to succeed instead.

This mind sharpener has 4 charges and does not recharge - a gift from the desert hermit Grit's collection.
