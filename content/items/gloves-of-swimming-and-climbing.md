---
name: Gloves of Swimming and Climbing
type: wondrous
rarity: uncommon
owner: mulchbane
origin: the-desert
---

While wearing these gloves, climbing and swimming don't cost extra movement, and the wearer gains a +5 bonus to Strength (Athletics) checks made to climb or swim.

A gift from the desert hermit Grit's collection, given to Mulchbane after the gnome proved himself in the cactid battle.
