A waterproof scroll case recovered from the bookshelves of the Tidefang. The case contained four scrolls, deciphered together by Petunia and Rowan:

- *Water Walk*
- *Augury*
- *Water Breathing*
- *Spider Climb*

A practical kit for a coastal voyage — and a record of the precautions S'shar's crew had taken before whatever was in the deep water came for them anyway.
