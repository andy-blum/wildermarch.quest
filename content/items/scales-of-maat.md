name: The Scales of Ma'at
type: wondrous
rarity: rare
owner: madame-helena-the-mystic
origin: sunken-pyramid

A bronze censer of polished silver and chain-fine workmanship, recovered from the sarcophagus that had been intended for Anu the Pyramid Cat. The censer was buried with the priest-cat's body in mind; it never made it to the grave it was meant for.

Anu demonstrated its use on the floor of the embalming chamber — a slow swing, a curl of smoke — and the party's wounds dimmed by its blessing.

Helena carries it.
