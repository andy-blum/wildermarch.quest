---
name: Wand of Secrets
type: wand
rarity: uncommon
origin: the-desert
---

A wand that pulses and points toward the nearest secret door or trap within 30 feet when activated.

This wand had 3 charges and did not recharge — a gift from the desert hermit Grit's collection, given to Jorath in exchange for his wand of fireballs. Jorath spent one charge identifying the workings of the Coffer of Equivalent Exchange in the Sunken Pyramid, then placed the wand itself on the offering platform as part of the exchange that yielded the Bracelet of the Cobra, the Bow of the Horizon, and the Ring of Mind Shielding.
