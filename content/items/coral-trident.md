A +1 trident forged of hardened coral, barbed with white coral arrowheads. This was Coralia's personal weapon, wielded by the ocean dryad for millennia in defense of her reef.

When massive tentacles rose from the deep during the reef rescue, Coralia hurled the trident onto the deck of the party's ship with her final words: "Go. You always have a friend on the reef."

Ozemon now carries the weapon and the charge that comes with it - a physical representation of his oath to help Coralia, wherever she may be.
