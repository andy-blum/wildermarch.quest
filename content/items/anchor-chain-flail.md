name: Brine Justice

A heavy flail fashioned from a ship's anchor and chain, recovered from the Coral Forest. Given to Aldric Vaun by Coralia as thanks for restoring her arm. Aldric named it Brine Justice.

The weapon flashes with smite-born fury when Aldric channels his divine oaths through it. It has been driven into necromancers beneath the Great Tree, aberrations on the reef, and the shadow entity at the Great Mushroom.
