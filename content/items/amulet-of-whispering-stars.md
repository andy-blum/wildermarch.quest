A star-shaped necklace bearing eyes in each point and a larger eye in the center - the symbol of the Cult of the Whispering Stars. One of the eyes matches the psionic aberration encountered in the corrupted cave exactly.

**Properties:**
- Cast Detect Thoughts once per long rest
- Telepathic communication with any visible creature, with no range limit
- Advantage on all Insight checks

**The Pattern:**
When combined with Thalen's starry cloak and mapped against the Wildermarch, the amulet revealed a deliberate pattern. The star points correspond to known cult activity sites: the cliffside obelisk, the corrupted cave, the reef, and the mountain cavern.

The largest eye on the amulet - its center - corresponds to an empty field beyond the outpost. Whatever the cult is building toward, that field may be its focus.

Recovered from a dead cultist in the corrupted cave. Madam Helena carries it.
