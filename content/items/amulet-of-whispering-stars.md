A star-shaped necklace bearing eyes in each point and a larger eye in the center - the symbol of the Cult of the Whispering Stars. One of the eyes matches the psionic aberration encountered in the corrupted cave exactly.

Recovered from a dead cultist in the corrupted cave. Madam Helena carries it.

**Properties:**
- Cast Detect Thoughts once per long rest
- Telepathic communication with any visible creature, with no range limit
- Advantage on all Insight checks

**The Pattern:**
When combined with Thalen's starry cloak and mapped against the Wildermarch, the amulet revealed a deliberate pattern. The star points correspond to known cult activity sites: the cliffside obelisk, the corrupted cave, the reef, and the mountain cavern.

The largest eye on the amulet — its center — corresponds to an empty field beyond the outpost. Whatever the cult is building toward, that field may be its focus.

**The Five Eyes:**
Silas, a captured cultist, revealed that each eye on the symbol represents a different entity.

> Each Eye is a truth. Each eye is a different ending, each worse than the last. It does not just watch the world, it chooses it.  It shapes the world around them to their fitting, and not to yours.
> Too many eyes to lie. Too many mouths to agree - they argue while they chew, none of them agreeing on what to eat. It remembers being everyone and no one all at once.
> A storm as tall as a mountain, with a face carved from the thunderheads. Run, if you like...the horizon belongs to it. It's wings create winds that lift cities, with a voice that splits skies.
> A beast larger than castles, than cities, than states. Drifting where there is no ground. It's jaws open into a darkness that does not end.  It's eye, wide and unblinking, seeing through worlds like a window.
> This one...this one is older then the rest. Older then all.  This saw everything be made.
