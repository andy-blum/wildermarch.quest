A star-shaped necklace bearing eyes in each point and a larger eye in the center. One of the eyes matches the psionic aberration encountered in the corrupted cave exactly.

**Properties:**
- Cast Detect Thoughts once per long rest
- Telepathic communication with any visible creature, with no range limit
- Advantage on all Insight checks

Recovered from the cultists in the corrupted cave.
