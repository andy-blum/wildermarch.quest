---
name: Mariner's Splint Armor
type: armor
rarity: uncommon
origin: northern-sea-cave
---

Heavy splint armor recovered from the stomach of the Roper during the sea cavern climb. This armor does not impose disadvantage on Stealth checks while swimming and grants a swimming speed equal to the wearer's walking speed.

Its previous owner, presumably consumed by the Roper, remains unknown.
