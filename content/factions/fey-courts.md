The political structure of the Feywild, divided between Summer and Winter courts in perpetual tension.

**Known Powers:**
- **Titania** - Queen of the Summer Court
- **Queen Mab** - Queen of the Winter Court

During negotiations with the party, Madam Helena's Detect Thoughts skimmed the surface of Autumn Laurel's mind, and two names surfaced repeatedly: Titania and Queen Mab. The tension was unmistakable - not deception, but unease.

The Fey Eladrin guardians of the Great Tree (Autumn Laurel, Solstice Briar, and Sun Shade) serve the Tree's interests, though their exact political allegiances remain unclear.

**Relationship to the Material Plane:**
The Eladrin claim the Shadowfell incursion at the Great Tree was the first of its kind, though Trogeth's testimony suggests otherwise. When asked about the cult and psionic creatures on the Material Plane, Autumn Laurel faltered, describing them as "mortal threats" he did not recognize - or could not admit to recognizing.
