The expedition organization that established the beachhead settlement in the Wildermarch. N.O.D.E. coordinates exploration efforts, assigns parties to investigate points of interest, and works to build a permanent presence in this uncharted territory.

**Notable members:**
- Captain Marinn (expedition coordinator)
- Captain Embercrest (shipwreck survivor)
- Chef Simon (head cook)
- Smith (foreman)
- Professor Fiddlesticks (artificer)
- Ahmed Noke (transmuter, on probation)

The organization's flag now flies above the growing settlement, a statement of intent against the wild horizon. A second site — the wreckage of Shinebright's Treehouse, north of the Great Tree — has been offered to N.O.D.E. as a forward facility or [Bastion](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1828-exploring-bastions-from-the-2024-dungeon-masters#What_is_a_Bastion), with the possibility of teleportation rooms eventually linking it to camp.
