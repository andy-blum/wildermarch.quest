The expedition organization that established the beachhead settlement in the Wildermarch. N.O.D.E. coordinates exploration efforts, assigns parties to investigate points of interest, and works to build a permanent presence in this uncharted territory.

**Notable members:**
- Captain Marinn (expedition coordinator)
- Captain Embercrest (shipwreck survivor)
- Chef Simon (head cook)
- Smith (foreman)
- Professor Fiddlesticks (artificer)

The organization's flag now flies above the growing settlement, a statement of intent against the wild horizon.
