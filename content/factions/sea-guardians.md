Ancient protectors of the coastal waters, now struggling against the corruption spreading through their domain. Some guardians have been twisted by the cult's influence.

**Known members:**
- Coralia (ocean dryad, ally)
- The Leviathan (corrupted, former guardian)
- Other unnamed guardians (status unknown)

Coralia has named the party "Reef Friends" and given them coral fragments as tokens of recognition. She promised to visit the camp to discuss the threats facing her domain.

The corruption coincides with N.O.D.E.'s arrival, though its origins predate the expedition. At least two leviathan-class entities exist beneath the coast.
