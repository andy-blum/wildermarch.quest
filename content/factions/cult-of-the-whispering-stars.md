A widespread cult operating throughout the Wildermarch, serving something vast beneath the waves. Members wear star-speckled robes and bear star-shaped symbols with eyes at each point.

**Activities:**
- Operating necromantic crystals that transform the dead into sea spawn
- Performing rituals to birth psionic aberrations
- Establishing outposts in caves and caverns across the region
- Willingly sacrificing themselves crying "I give myself to you!"

**Known sites:**
- Corrupted cave (destroyed)
- Mountain cavern (destroyed)
- Coral Forest crystal (destroyed)
- Underwater tower (intact, location known)

A letter recovered from cultists mentioned reinforcements delayed by lizardfolk attacks in a swamp, suggesting ongoing conflict with other factions and additional cult presence in unexplored areas.

The stars are watching.
