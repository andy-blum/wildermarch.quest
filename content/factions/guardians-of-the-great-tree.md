name: Guardians of the Great Tree
disposition: friendly

The fey protectors of the Great Tree, which exists across the Material Plane, the Feywild, and the Shadowfell. The guardians work to maintain the Tree's health and the barrier between planes.

**Members:**
- Chweech and Chwong (chwinga guides)
- Numerous other chwingas
- Autumn Laurel (Fey Eladrin)
- Solstice Briar (Fey Eladrin)
- Sun Shade (Fey Eladrin)

The chwingas communicate through gestures and have formed a bond with the expedition after the party helped heal the Tree's corruption. They have gifted runed stones, golden acorns, and access to the Feywild pocket within the Tree.

The Eladrin have formally invited the party to return for further reward.
