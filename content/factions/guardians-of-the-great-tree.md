The fey protectors of the Great Tree, which exists across the Material Plane, the Feywild, and the Shadowfell. The guardians work to maintain the Tree's health and the barrier between planes.

**Known Members:**
- **Chweech and Chwong** - Chwinga guides who have formed a close bond with the party
- Numerous other chwingas
- **Autumn Laurel** - Fey Eladrin, speaks for the guardians
- **Solstice Briar** - Fey Eladrin, thoughts echo repeatedly with "Shadowfell"
- **Sun Shade** - Fey Eladrin

**The Chwinga:**
The chwingas communicate through gestures and have formed a deep bond with the expedition. They led the party to discover the corruption, guided them through the Tree's interior, and embraced as the wood began to heal. More chwingas now gather, placing their hands against the walls as golden light spreads through the roots.

**The Eladrin:**
The three Eladrin manifested in a temporary pocket of the Feywild to explain the Tree's nature - an axis touching three realms where direct contact between Feywild and Shadowfell should be impossible. They revealed the existence of two other anchor points (the Great Pine and Great Mushroom) and promised further reward for restoration of the portal network.

The chwingas carved the cult pendant's likeness into bark and handed it to Autumn, who promised to consult his brother.

**Gifts Given:**
- Runed stones of heroism
- Golden acorns (one-time use Blink)
- Access to the Feywild pocket within the Tree
- A lock of Autumn Laurel's golden hair (to Thalen)
