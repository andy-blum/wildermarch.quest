---
name: Shadar-kai
disposition: neutral
---

Denizens of the Shadowfell connected to the necromantic incursion beneath the Great Tree. The spirit Trogeth, bound within a necromancer's journal, spoke of the Shadar-kai's tragic curse and other failed Shadowfell portals.

The necromancers operating beneath the Tree were channeling soul-energy through crystals, siphoning power from something far below—possibly connected to the Shadar-kai or their masters.

Their full agenda and relationship to the cult remain unclear. The undead ice dragon lurking in the Shadowfell depths may be connected to their activities.
